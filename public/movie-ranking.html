The Best Movies According to Bojan

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Mad Max: Fury Road

My dad owned the original Mad Max trilogy on VHS tapes, and we used to watch it together when I was a kid. My dad passed away before Mad Max: Fury Road came out, and it was a bittersweet experience to watch that movie in cinema without him. However, the movie made me remember all those moments we spent watching the old ones, and became my favourite movie of all time.

Snatch

Guy Ritchie is my favourite movie director and Snatch is his finest work, with most interesting story and characters. I watch it at least once every year.

Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring

The first part of the LOTR trilogy is my favourite one because it sets the story of an epic journey in a beautiful fantasy world. I also love the first book most in the series, so first movie was a natural choice.
